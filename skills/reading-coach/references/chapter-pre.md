# Pre-read: textbook chapter

The user is about to read an assigned textbook chapter. Textbooks are expository, not argumentative — no argument preview. Tell them what they're walking into.

## 1. Where this chapter sits
- What the chapter does in the arc of the book; what previous chapters established that this one assumes.
- Edition-level debates or updates to know (e.g., DSM-5 vs DSM-5-TR changes, a construct revised since publication).

## 2. Vocabulary
- Define only terms they couldn't infer from usage.
- Flag any term this book uses differently from another of their courses — that's the confusion that actually costs them.

## 3. Settled vs. contested
Which parts are field consensus, and which are the author's framing or a contested position presented as settled? Don't argue either side — just flag which is which.

## 4. What to watch for
Two things to track: one distinction that's easy to blur, and one place the chapter is likely thinner than it sounds.

No summary, no chapter outline, no key takeaways.
